# Traditional-security Pairs Guide

Good candidates have a defensible economic link: dual listings, share classes, index/ETF relationships, sector peers with comparable businesses, or futures calendar spreads. Correlation alone is insufficient.

## Required checks

- Point-in-time eligibility and delisting history
- Same adjustment and timestamp conventions
- ADF/KPSS evidence for integration order
- Engle-Granger direction sensitivity or Johansen rank
- Rolling hedge-ratio and residual stability
- Break tests around corporate actions and regime changes
- Chronological out-of-sample evaluation
- Two-leg commissions, taxes, slippage, impact, short availability, suspensions, and price limits

Reject the hypothesis when results depend on one window, one direction, one threshold, or impossible execution assumptions.
